Project Flow
How FlavorFind Works

A simple, delightful journey from landing to cooking.

1

Open FlavorFind

The Home Page loads with a search bar, category filters, and a warm sliding photo gallery.

2

Find or Generate Recipes

Search by ingredient, use Voice Search, upload a photo, or let the AI Generator craft something unique.

3

JavaScript Filters Results

Client-side JS instantly narrows down the recipe list — no server round-trip, lightning fast.

4

Enter Cook Mode

Click any recipe to open the full detail modal, then launch Step-by-Step Cook Mode with live timer.

5

Check Nutrition

Use the Nutrition Calculator to estimate calories and macros from your ingredient list.

6

Save to Favourites

Hit the heart icon — stored in LocalStorage and lives on your Favourites page forever.
